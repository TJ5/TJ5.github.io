The hallmark of a good plot twist is that it delivers something the reader didn't know they wanted. It shouldn't just be shocking for the sake of shock value but rather should feel plausible yet also improve the story for its inclusion. Every single twist (of which there were many) added to this story. From the Aranea's plans of betrayal, to the reveal as to the true nature of the time loop, to Panaxeth revealing itself on the cusp of Zach and Zorian's victory, to Quatach-Ichl meeting them in a human guise out of the blue, and of course the final twist with Zach's angelic contract, everything added to the story. This is not even an exhaustive list, yet the point carries.
