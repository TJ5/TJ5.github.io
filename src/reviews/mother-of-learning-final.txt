I absolutely loved Mother of Learning, to the extent that it almost felt wrong that the whole story should be available for free. The only reason I do not give it a perfect 10/10 is that I wish there was more of it, and the story left off in a place that left it unambiguously completed yet also hinted that Zach and Zorian had many more adventures ahead of them. I strongly recommend this series to anyone looking for a fantasy series that is a bit different to what they may previously have been familiar with, if they were more familiar with mainstream published titles.

Final Rating: 9/10.
